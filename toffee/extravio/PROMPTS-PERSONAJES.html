Prompts de personajes

Imagen y voz de los cuatro personajes fijos. Cada bloque tiene su botón de Copiar.

Orden recomendado

  1. Imágenes primero, en Nano Banana Pro: Dizzy, la abuela, Tocino y Bartolo. Saca 2 o 3 versiones de cada uno y me las mandas para escoger.
  2. Voces después, en ElevenLabs (Diseño de voz): pega la descripción, genera con el texto de prueba, saca 2 o 3 y me mandas la que más te guste. Guarda cada una con el nombre del personaje.
  3. Las cuatro imágenes deben verse del mismo mundo: por eso todas llevan el mismo estilo al final del prompt.

Dizzy

La rata protagonista. Adjunta LUCHO.jpg para que no cambie la cara.

Imagen (Nano Banana Pro)
Character reference sheet of the exact same rat from the reference image, four views (front, three-quarter, side, back) on a plain light gray background: a skinny gray rat with scruffy fur, huge bloodshot eyes with dark circles, big pink ears with small nicks, buck teeth, a small shaved line in one eyebrow. He wears a flat-brim baseball cap tilted to the side with a small scratched black action camera mounted on top of the cap, a thick fake gold chain with a soda bottle cap as the pendant, a torn black hoodie, white socks pulled up high with black slide sandals, and an old olive canvas backpack with many rusty keys hanging from it. Cocky street pose with his chest out. In one view he holds a selfie stick made from an old umbrella handle. Photorealistic 3D animated film style, same face, proportions and colors as the reference, no text, no letters, no logos on the cap or clothes.
Voz (ElevenLabs, Diseño de voz)
Speaks only in Colombian Spanish with a rough street accent from a working-class Colombian neighborhood, clear enough for any Spanish speaker to understand. A skinny cocky street kid in his early twenties with a naturally raspy, slightly hoarse and nasal voice, fast and full of swagger, drags some words when he is showing off, talks like the neighborhood hustler bragging to his friends on the corner. Very expressive: goes from whispering secrets to shouting in surprise, with a quick nervous wheezy giggle and a soft whistle when he breathes in. Funny, cheeky, overconfident and a little paranoid. Natural and realistic, recorded close to a good microphone, not a cartoon, not a narrator, not an announcer.
Texto de prueba
¿Quiubo pues, mis ratas? Todo bien, todo bien. Hoy me voy pa'l páramo con Bartolo… si es que este animal se quiere mover. ¡Bartolo! Nada, mi llave, qué visaje. Je, je. Pilas, y no le digan a mi cucha que me robé la panela.

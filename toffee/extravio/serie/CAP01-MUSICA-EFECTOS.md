# Capítulo 1 · parte 5: música y efectos

## Música

### M1 · La finca (bambuco suave)

Escenas 3, 4 y 16 · 1 minuto · instrumental

```
Gentle Colombian Andean bambuco, instrumental only: tiple and acoustic guitar with soft bandola, warm and cozy like sunrise on a coffee farm in the mountains, light and happy, slow-medium tempo, no drums, no vocals.
```

### M2 · El camino con Bartolo (carranga)

Escena 5 · 45 s · instrumental

```
Upbeat Colombian carranga, instrumental only: requinto, tiple, guitar and guacharaca, playful and bouncy like a mule trotting down a mountain trail, funny countryside mood, no vocals.
```

### M3 · La plaza del pueblo (vallenato)

Escena 6 · 45 s · instrumental

```
Cheerful Colombian vallenato, instrumental only: accordion, caja drum and guacharaca, sunny small-town square mood, relaxed medium tempo, good for background under dialogue, no vocals.
```

### M4 · LA CANCIÓN: «Bienvenido a El Hueco» (champeta, con letra)

Escena 8 · 30 a 35 s · CON VOZ. Pega el estilo y la letra (abajo).

```
Catchy Colombian champeta, fast African-Caribbean guitar riffs, heavy bass and drums, street party in a dangerous neighborhood at night. Lead singer: sassy Caribbean Colombian woman. Short call-and-response with rough male voices. Funny, mischievous and dirty mood. Spanish lyrics.
```

aquí se entra, pero no se sale.

### M5 · El Tuerto (reguetón oscuro)

Escenas 2, 10 y 13 · 1 minuto · instrumental

```
Slow dark reggaeton instrumental, deep heavy bass, minimal dembow beat, menacing and mysterious like a small-time gangster in a dark alley, comic villain vibe, no vocals.
```

### M6 · Cuca coquetea (saxofón)

Escena 12 · 15 s · instrumental

```
Cheesy, exaggerated sexy saxophone solo, comedic romantic seduction music, slow and funny, no vocals.
```

### M7 · La apuesta del dominó (tensión)

Escena 13 · 30 s · instrumental

```
Tense suspense music with slow Colombian tambora and alegre drums, building tension like a high-stakes game, a ticking feeling, no vocals.
```

### M8 · Llega la abuela (western)

Escena 14 · 20 s · instrumental

```
Spaghetti western showdown music: lonely whistle, twangy guitar and a slow dramatic build, a duel is about to happen, epic and funny, no vocals.
```

### M9 · El regreso al atardecer (bambuco tierno)

Escenas 15 y 16 · 1 minuto · instrumental

```
Slow, tender Colombian bambuco, instrumental only: tiple, guitar and soft strings, emotional sunset in the mountains, warm family love, a little nostalgic, no drums, no vocals.
```

### M10 · El misterio de la cámara (suspenso)

Escena 17 · 20 s · instrumental

```
Eerie magical mystery music: low electric hum, soft glitchy synth pads and a few music-box notes, something strange is happening at night, no vocals.
```

### M11 · El tema de Extravío (cierre y pantallas finales)

Escena 18 y los 16 s finales · 30 s · instrumental

```
Fun signature theme for an animated comedy series: Colombian cumbia rhythm mixed with a punk rock electric guitar riff, catchy and mischievous, upbeat, ends with a clear final hit, no vocals.
```
